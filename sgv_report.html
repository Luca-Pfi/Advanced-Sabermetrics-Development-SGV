Starter Game Value

A pitcher's win-loss record depends on run support and the bullpen. SGV gives every start a win, loss or no-decision on the pitching alone, against a league-average offense and bullpen, adjusted for the ballpark. A start is an SGV no-decision when it left the team a chance to win.

SGV = (ER + Bullpen RA9 × (9 − IP) / 9) − (Runs per team game × Park factor)

Player Search

Every starter, qualified or not

    Method

    Qualified: at least 1 start per 8 team games (20 GS over a full season). Swing = (SGV W − SGV L) − (real W − real L).

    Data

    Box scores: MLB Stats API. Park factors: FanGraphs.
